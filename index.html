Questions people ask

How do I know which pump my car needs?

Choose your car in the finder, or send us your make, model, year and engine size on WhatsApp. Sending the old pump or a photo of its connector helps us confirm the fit.

Do you deliver?

Ask us on WhatsApp about delivery. We will tell you what is available and any fees before you pay.

Is the price fixed?

Prices depend on stock and fitment. We confirm the price with you on WhatsApp before you pay.

What if my car is not listed?

Send us the details anyway. We check each request, and some pumps fit more cars than their listing shows.
