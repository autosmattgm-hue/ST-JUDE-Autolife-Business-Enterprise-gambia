A shop that does one thing well

ST JUDE Autolife Business Enterprise is a Gambian business focused on car fuel pumps. Our range is made up of reconditioned imported pumps from Europe, America and Japan. We focus on the parts that keep an engine running: in-tank pumps, external inline pumps, diesel lift pumps, complete modules and the relays that drive them.

Questions people ask

How do I know which pump my car needs?

Choose your car in the finder, or send us your make, model, year and engine size on WhatsApp. Sending the old pump or a photo of its connector helps us confirm the fit.

Do you deliver?

Ask us on WhatsApp about delivery. We will tell you what is available and any fees before you pay.

Is the price fixed?

Prices depend on stock and fitment. We confirm the price with you on WhatsApp before you pay.

When are you open?

We are open 24 hours from Monday through Saturday. Call or message us before you come so we can prepare the correct pump.

What if my car is not listed?

Send us the details anyway. We check each request, and some pumps fit more cars than their listing shows.
